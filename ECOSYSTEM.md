# Trust Union Ecosystem

Trust Union connects **normative patterns**, **machine-verifiable validation** and **delivery** without mixing responsibilities.

## Roles of the core projects
The **ecosystem of the Trust Union** consists of:

### Trust Union (this organization)
**Trust Union** defines **what must be true** in regulated, publicly funded digital artifacts:  
neutral, evidence-based requirement and security-by-design patterns.

### DSL-Core
dsl-core defines **how patterns can be encoded and validated deterministically**:  
machine-readable contracts (profiles/schemas) plus parsing and normalization.

### Agile Innovation Framework (AIF)
AIF defines **how work is delivered by iterative agile prototyping**: work packages, roles, iteration mechanics, and how review and quality gates should be embbeded.  
It is the execution model to turn patterns into real-world outcomes.



## Principle
- Trust Union Patterns = normative content focussed on it security 
- DSL-Core = formalization & deterministic validation contracts
- AIF = delivery mechanics  

Each layer is intentionally separated to protect neutrality, interoperability, and long-term trust.
